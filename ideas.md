# LyroWeb Pricing Redesign — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### 1. The Midnight Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark editorial pricing catalogue with warm metallic signals and an assured, quietly premium rhythm. The experience treats every package as a considered service offering rather than a generic card.
**Probability:** 0.06

### 2. Paper Signals
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile light-mode art-book layout built from paper textures, ink rules, and kinetic cut-out shapes. It would make the agency feel human and craft-led.
**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Studio Index
**Very Brief Intro:** A sharply structured Swiss-inspired systems page with a high-contrast grid and restrained technical motion. It would communicate operational clarity and directness.
**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen approach: The Midnight Ledger

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial art direction meets high-end product marketing: a refined digital price catalogue with the tactile restraint of a fashion lookbook and the precision of a modern studio portfolio.

### Core Principles
1. Let the pricing be the hero: typographic hierarchy and clear information should feel as deliberate as the artwork.
2. Use measured contrast: ivory typography and saffron details provide warmth against ink-black surfaces.
3. Turn interaction into affordance: cards, buttons, and navigation acknowledge a visitor's attention through short, physical motion.
4. Keep every visual gesture purposeful: no decorative animation competes with scanning, comparison, or conversion.

### Color Philosophy
The background is a nearly black green-ink (#0c110f) to feel distinctive from generic charcoal interfaces and to provide a calm stage for the content. Bone ivory (#f3f0e8) carries the typography with a printed-page softness. Saffron gold (#d8b44b) is reserved for active choices, numeric emphasis, and the clearest routes to contact, making it feel earned rather than ornamental.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is composed as a drifting editorial ledger rather than a centrally stacked marketing landing page. A left-weighted hero opens into staggered service sections. On larger screens, every pricing collection has a narrow vertical rail for its label and a wide content field for the packages; mobile collapses this into an intentional single-column reading sequence.

### Signature Elements
1. Thin saffron ledger rules with small monospaced service labels.
2. Soft rectangular image windows that crop bespoke abstract studio objects.
3. A precise angled corner mark that appears in cards, buttons, and section transitions.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are composed to reward exploration without disrupting comparison. Cards lift a few pixels and reveal a faint internal light on hover; feature lines brighten progressively; buttons slide their arrow forward and compress slightly on press. Navigation links receive a restrained underline draw.

### Animation
Entrance motion uses opacity and a 12–18px upward drift in small staggered groups. The hero artwork has a low-amplitude floating drift and quiet parallax-like shift. Hover transitions sit between 140ms and 240ms with a sharp ease-out; no interaction should require waiting. All non-essential animation is disabled under reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Cormorant Garamond is the expressive display voice for package names and persuasive headlines, using high contrast italics sparingly for emphasis. DM Mono provides the utilitarian labels, pricing suffixes, and service metadata. Manrope is reserved for reading copy and feature lists, with compact letter spacing and generous line-height.

### Brand Essence
**LyroWeb Solutions is the straight-talking digital studio for growing businesses that want visibly considered work with clear, upfront pricing.**

Personality: **considered, candid, capable**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are decisive and descriptive, CTAs are specific verbs, and microcopy makes scope and value easy to understand. Avoid vague motivational claims.

Example lines:

> Design with a clear price and a clear point of view.

> Choose the right build, then let’s map the details.

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the generated angular saffron ribbon mark as the primary visual identifier. Pair it with a small tracked uppercase wordmark that reads like a stamped studio imprint, not a default app header.

**Ledger Saffron — #D8B44B**
